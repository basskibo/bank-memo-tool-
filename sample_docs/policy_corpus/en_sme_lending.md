# SME Lending Standards (SYNTHETIC — not real SCB policy)

## Tenor limits

SME working-capital facilities are granted for a maximum tenor of 36 months, renewable on
annual review. SME term loans for capital expenditure may run up to 84 months including a
grace period that must not exceed 12 months. Overdraft and revolving facilities are uncommitted
and reviewed at least once every 12 months.

## Facility size

The total SME exposure to a single obligor group must not exceed EGP 50,000,000 without
escalation to the Head-Office Credit Committee. Facilities above EGP 20,000,000 require two
years of audited financial statements.

## Pricing floor

SME facilities are priced at a minimum of the Central Bank of Egypt corridor rate plus 300
basis points. Any pricing below this floor is an exception requiring Credit Committee approval
and must be recorded in the credit file.
