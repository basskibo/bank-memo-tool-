# Credit Risk Ratio Thresholds (SYNTHETIC — not real SCB policy)

## Debt service coverage

The minimum debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) for a new term facility is 1.25 times, measured
on projected operating cash flow over scheduled principal and interest. Borrowers with a DSCR
between 1.10 and 1.25 may be approved only with additional collateral or a shorter tenor, and
the deviation must be justified in writing.

## Leverage

Net debt to EBITDA must not exceed 4.0 times at approval for a leveraged borrower, or 3.0 times
for project finance. The current ratio (current assets over current liabilities) should be at
least 1.1 times; a ratio below 1.0 is a red flag requiring Credit Committee attention.

## Interest cover

The interest coverage ratio (EBIT over finance costs) must be at least 2.0 times. A borrower
whose interest cover falls below 1.5 times during the life of the facility is placed on the
watch list and reviewed quarterly.
